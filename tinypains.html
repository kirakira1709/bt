I feel a lot, therefore tiny things cut me deeply.

It’s like that minuscule fragment of broken glass stuck in your thumbprint,

which we spent a nonsensical amount of time searching for,

only to fail utterly.

That’s the glimmer of the devilish thing.

It decided to nest under my skin,

stay rent-free for the rest of my eternity,

and never let my consciousness sleep.

Not a night.

As if pure joy could compensate for a childish prank.
